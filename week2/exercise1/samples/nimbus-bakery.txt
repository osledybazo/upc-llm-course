Nimbus Bakery — company notes (fictional)

Nimbus Bakery is a small bakery cooperative founded in March 2017 in Vilanova i la Geltrú, a coastal town south of Barcelona. It was started by three friends: Laia Puig, a former chemistry teacher; Omar Haddad, a pastry chef trained in Lyon; and Jordi Serra, who ran the books. Laia Puig has been the cooperative's president since 2021.

The bakery is best known for its "cloud loaf", a sourdough made with 30% spelt flour and a 36-hour cold fermentation. The cloud loaf costs 4.80 euros and weighs 750 grams. The second best seller is the carquinyoli almond biscuit, sold in 200-gram bags for 3.50 euros.

Nimbus has two shops. The original shop is on Carrer de la Unió and opens from 7:00 to 14:00, Tuesday to Sunday; it is closed on Mondays. The second shop opened in October 2023 next to the train station and also opens on Monday afternoons, from 16:00 to 20:00.

The cooperative has 18 members and 6 non-member employees. Every member has one vote at the yearly assembly, which is always held on the first Saturday of February. New members must work at the bakery for at least one year before they can join, and they pay an entry fee of 1,500 euros.

All flour comes from a single mill, Molí de Can Ribes, in the Penedès region, about 25 kilometres away. Nimbus uses no palm oil, and since 2022 all its bread bags are made from recycled paper. Unsold bread is given every evening to a local food bank called Rebost Solidari.

In 2024 Nimbus Bakery had a turnover of 1.2 million euros and baked roughly 410,000 loaves. Its oven is a wood-fired oven built in 1962, bought from a closing bakery in Sitges and rebuilt brick by brick. The staff nicknamed the oven "La Vella" (the old lady).

Customers can order cakes for collection at least 48 hours in advance by phone. Nimbus does not offer home delivery. Gluten-free products are not available, because the cooperative cannot guarantee a flour-free environment in its kitchens.
